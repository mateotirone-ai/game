# Trade

## Intent

Trade turns local surplus into regional power: caravans, contracts, tariffs, smuggling risk, and market discovery.

## Loops

1. Produce surplus in a specialty.
2. Discover demand (posted orders, AI demand, player contracts).
3. Move goods — **player haul early**; **caravan routes/contracts unlock later** (see `Design/Decisions/20260810_trade-caravans.md`).
4. Clear market; pay tariffs / guards / losses.
5. Reinvest or stockpile strategic goods.

## Instruments

- **Spot sales** at settlement markets.
- **Contracts** — Delivery by date/qty/quality.
- **Trade routes** — Semi-persistent paths with risk and upkeep.
- **Embargoes / tariffs** — Driven by Diplomacy / Government.

## Risk

Bandits, spoilage, war zones, and poor roads create the need for escorts, better packaging tech, and diplomatic corridors.
