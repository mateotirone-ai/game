# Design Pillars

These pillars arbitrate feature tradeoffs. If a proposal violates a pillar, revise or reject it.

## 1. Immortal agency

The player is a continuous character in a persistent world. Death, defeat, and setbacks are events—not resets of identity. Consequences stick.

## 2. Systems over scripts

Prefer interlocking rules (economy, logistics, politics, tech) that create emergent stories. Hand-authored set pieces support systems; they do not replace them.

## 3. Village → city is the campaign

The core emotional arc is settlement scale: camp → village → town → city. Features should either feed that growth or create meaningful tension against it.

## 4. Honest multiplayer

Design for dedicated authoritative servers even while prototyping in single-player. No “SP-only” economy or inventory that cannot replicate later.

## 5. Controller-first clarity

UI, camera, and interactions assume gamepad first, keyboard/mouse second. Stylized art supports readability at gameplay distances.

## 6. Regional identity

Places specialize. Ore valleys, grain plains, and port towns should feel economically and visually distinct—and depend on each other.

## 7. Conflict with consent gradients

Cooperation is default-viable. Conflict is real—raids, sanctions, wars—but gated by readable rules, diplomacy, and democratic / legal constraints where settlements adopt them.
