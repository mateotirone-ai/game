# Decision: Capital + outposts (map spread)

- Status: **Accepted**
- Date: 2026-08-10
- Related: Multiplayer Eve world, land-parcels, settlements, war declare

## Decision

### Player footprint

- A player has **one capital** settlement (full Homestead→City path).
- They may found **lesser outposts** to **spread and take more of the map**.

### Outpost capabilities

- Stage-capped (about **Homestead / Hamlet max**) — gather, storage, basic shelter/workshop, not a second full civ.
- Purpose: territorial reach + extractables feeding the capital (and trade).

### Taking map (**B — hard claim**)

- Outpost projects a **hard claim**: land parcels / extractors in the claim are yours to control.
- Others contest via **war declaration** / security-band rules (not casual grief inside hard claim).
- Aligns with Eve-like territory pressure without every player running five full cities.

### Sequencing

| Phase | Scope |
|-------|--------|
| First Playable | Single capital sandbox only |
| Next | Outpost foundation + hard claim stub + haul to capital |
| Later | Contested claims, war over outposts, logistics |

## Consequences

- UI: capital vs outpost distinction; claim map overlay.
- Balance: outpost caps prevent multi-city snowball; hard claims create diplomacy/war content.
