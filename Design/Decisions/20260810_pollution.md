# Decision: Pollution & Environment

- Status: **Accepted**
- Date: 2026-08-11
- Related: Economy, Water, Food chain, World, Building, civ meters

## Context

Creative chose **real pollution (C)** — industry can stress land/water; cleanup and tech matter.

## Decision

- Production and extractives can create **pollution / soil / water stress** that hits farms, health/hydration pressure, morale, and civ meters.
- Not cosmetic smog-only: unread pressure should be **mechanically felt**.
- **Mitigation** — placement (downwind/downstream), cleanup buildings/jobs, tech upgrades, zoning — is part of the industrial fantasy.
- Ties to parked irrigation/water: polluted water sources can degrade irrigation and hydration quality when those systems are live.
- Megaworld: same rules for fairness; private sliders may scale pollution intensity (`20260810_difficulty.md`).

### Sequencing

| Phase | Scope |
|-------|--------|
| First Playable | None |
| Next | Light local waste/smoke tell without full sim |
| Mid | Pollution meters + farm/water penalties; basic cleanup |
| Later | Watershed flow, regional fallout, advanced scrubbers |

### Authority

Pollution state and its production penalties are **server-authoritative**.
