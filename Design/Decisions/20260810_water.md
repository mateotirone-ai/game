# Decision: Water & Hydration

- Status: **Accepted** (irrigation locked; hydration severity still open)
- Date: 2026-08-10
- Related: World, Economy, Building, CoreGameplayLoop, Combat (survival pressure)

## Context

Creative chose **full water simulation** and called out that this is a **huge buildout**. **Hydration** is an intentional player mechanic, not flavor UI. Irrigation should feel **Minecraft-adjacent but more realistic**: dig channels that actually flow from a water source.

## Decision

### Settlement / world water (full sim intent)

Water is a real resource layer:

- **Sources** — rivers, coasts, lakes, rainfall catchment, wells/aquifers (data-driven by biome). Water must originate somewhere readable on the map.
- **Access** — riverfront / irrigated lots outperform dry lots for farms and some industry.
- **Infrastructure** — wells, cisterns, dug irrigation, later aqueducts (stage- and tech-gated).
- **Stress** — drought / low source level can dry channels and hit food, growth meters, and industry throughput.
- **Logistics** — water-adjacent sites matter for founding and trade (ports later).

This is **not** First Playable scope as a complete sim. It is a long-horizon systems pillar with phased delivery.

### Irrigation (creative lock)

- Players **dig irrigation channels** (terrain/trench tool — not magic “wet farmland” paint).
- Channels **flow from a real water source** (river, lake, spring, pumped well/cistern outlet). No floating infinite water without a source.
- Flow is **realistic enough to read**: downhill / connected path from source → fields; blocked/broken channels stop water; dry source = dry ditch.
- Minecraft kinship: simple place/dig vernacular and farm wetting. Divergence: **hydrology from source**, not Minecraft’s infinite-source quirks as the end-state fantasy.
- Wet fields (connected to flowing irrigation or natural floodplain) get farm bonuses / viability; unirrigated dry lots underperform or fail in arid biomes.

Exact cell resolution, pump tech, and evaporation rules are data/tuning later — authority stays on server.

### Player hydration

**Hydration is a survival mechanic** (thirst / water need). Exact drain, death, and refill rules are open (see open questions). Structural intent: readable on controller HUD; ties into wells, canteens/containers, biomes, and settlement water access over time.

### Sequencing (honest about size)

| Phase | Scope |
|-------|--------|
| First Playable | Visual water in sandbox OK; **no** channel hydrology. Optional: tiny thirst stub only if it reinforces settler fantasy without blocking gather/build exit criteria |
| Next | Drink from well/river + personal hydration meter; simple well building |
| Mid | Dig channels + source→flow→farm wetting; cistern storage |
| Later | Aquifers, drought events, pumps/aqueducts, ports, industry water use, map water pressure |

### First Playable stance

Keep FP exit criteria on gather/build/Homestead→Hamlet. Water/hydration **must not** gate the slice until the homestead loop is fun. Full sim ships in layers after that.

## Consequences

- Building/tools: ditch/irrigation tool + Well → Cistern → pump/outlet → Aqueduct family, stage/tech gated.
- Farm Plot tiers that mention “Irrigated farm” require connected flowing channels (or natural water adjacency).
- Biome founding pressure: dry biomes need wells + channel work early; river biomes start softer.
- Security/food crisis meters can include water shortage.
- Multiplayer: channel network, source levels, and drought are **server-authoritative**.

## Open questions (interview)

1. Hydration severity — soft debuff → death, or soft-only?
2. Always-on drain vs heat/exertion/biome modifiers?
3. Can you bottle/haul water as an inventory item early?
4. Is settlement water a shared civic pool, per-source rights, or free-for-all on common land?
