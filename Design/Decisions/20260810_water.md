# Decision: Water & Hydration

- Status: **Accepted** (hydration severity locked; irrigation depth still soft)
- Date: 2026-08-10
- Related: World, Economy, Building, CoreGameplayLoop, Combat (survival pressure)

## Context

Creative chose **full water simulation** and called out that this is a **huge buildout**. **Hydration** is an intentional player mechanic, not flavor UI. Irrigation direction: dig channels from real sources — **exact play feel still open**.

## Decision

### Settlement / world water (full sim intent)

Water is a real resource layer:

- **Sources** — rivers, coasts, lakes, rainfall catchment, wells/aquifers (data-driven by biome). Water must originate somewhere readable on the map.
- **Access** — riverfront / irrigated lots outperform dry lots for farms and some industry.
- **Infrastructure** — wells, cisterns, dug irrigation, later aqueducts (stage- and tech-gated).
- **Stress** — drought / low source level can dry channels and hit food, growth meters, and industry throughput.
- **Logistics** — water-adjacent sites matter for founding and trade (ports later).

This is **not** First Playable scope as a complete sim. It is a long-horizon systems pillar with phased delivery.

### Irrigation (direction only — not final rules)

**Agreed so far:**

- Prefer **dug channels** fed by a **real source** (not magic wet-paint farmland as the fantasy).
- Should feel somewhat **Minecraft-simple to author**, more **IRL-honest about flow from a source**.

**Still undecided (creative):** how deep the hydrology toy is — toy ditch network vs fuller gravity/gates/upstream politics. Do not over-lock until creative picks a lane.

### Player hydration

**Hydration is a survival mechanic.** Severity: **contextual (C)** —

- Mild thirst pressure in temperate / settled play.
- Dangerous mainly under **heat, arid biomes, and hard exertion**.
- Long ignore in those contexts can kill (soft → hard), not a constant Minecraft-hunger nag everywhere.

Readable on controller HUD; refill via natural water, wells, later canteens/containers.

### Sequencing (honest about size)

| Phase | Scope |
|-------|--------|
| First Playable | Visual water in sandbox OK; **no** channel hydrology. Optional: tiny thirst stub only if it reinforces settler fantasy without blocking gather/build exit criteria |
| Next | Drink from well/river + personal hydration meter (contextual drain); simple well building |
| Mid | First irrigation pass once creative locks depth; cistern storage |
| Later | Drought events, pumps/aqueducts, ports, industry water, map water pressure |

### First Playable stance

Keep FP exit criteria on gather/build/Homestead→Hamlet. Water/hydration **must not** gate the slice until the homestead loop is fun. Full sim ships in layers after that.

## Consequences

- Building/tools eventually include ditch tools + Well → Cistern → pump/outlet → Aqueduct family.
- Farm “Irrigated” tiers assume some source-connected water story (exact rules TBD).
- Biome founding pressure: arid starts lean harder on wells + hydration.
- Multiplayer: water state is **server-authoritative**.
