# Decision: Lore & Narrative

- Status: **Accepted**
- Date: 2026-08-10
- Related: first-playable (journal/era foresight), Vision, Progression, Multiplayer

## Context

Creative chose **heavy narrative (C)** with a critical twist: **create your own storyline** — not a fixed authored main-quest railroad.

## Decision

- Story weight is **high**: the game should feel like you’re living an epic civilization tale, not a mute sandbox.
- The **storyline is player/settlement-authored** — your founding, crises, wars, treaties, fortunes, and failures *are* the campaign.
- Support with **narrative instruments**, not a mandatory chapter questline:
  - Journal / era foresight (already in FP)
  - Diegetic chronicles, monuments, graves, town history log
  - Optional world myth crumbs (ruins, exemplars) that hint at eras — context, not a compulsory plot
- **No** required main-quest spine that every player must complete the same way.
- Multiplayer: many overlapping player storylines in one Eve-like world; chronicles should credit actors without forcing shared cutscene rails.

### Sequencing

| Phase | Scope |
|-------|--------|
| First Playable | Journal + diegetic “what towns become” tell |
| Next | Auto town chronicle (stage-ups, wars, big builds) |
| Mid | Player monuments / inscriptions; richer history UI |
| Later | Deeper myth layer + shareable chronicle exports if desired |

## Locked interview answers

1. Narrative → **C + player-authored storyline** (heavy feel; no fixed main quest)
