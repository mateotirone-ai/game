# Decision: Culture & Religion

- Status: **Accepted** (reversed — **no culture/religion systems**)
- Date: 2026-08-10
- Related: Government, Building, World, Pillars

## Context

Creative initially chose full culture + religion systems (**C**), then **reversed**: **no culture and faith** as gameplay systems.

## Decision

**Do not ship culture or religion as systems.**

- No faith affiliations, temples-as-faith mechanics, schisms, or culture cohesion layers.
- No player-founded or preset faith/culture gameplay loops.
- Towns still get **regional architecture / unique look** from existing art-direction locks — that is place identity, not a culture/religion sim.
- Civic buildings that look ceremonial (plaza, monument, journal shrine) stay **civic / diegetic / progression**, not religious mechanics.

If culture/faith is ever revisited, it requires a **new** Decision — this one closes the topic as out of scope.
