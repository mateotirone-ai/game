# Decision: Culture & Religion

- Status: **Accepted** (full systems intent; rule details pending)
- Date: 2026-08-10
- Related: Government, Building, World (regional identity), Diplomacy, Pillars

## Context

Creative chose **full systems (C)** for both **culture** and **religion** — cohesion, conflict, buildings, and laws, not cosmetics-only.

## Decision

Culture and religion are **first-class gameplay systems**:

- **Culture** — regional / settlement identity (language of place, customs, architectural lean already partly locked). Affects cohesion, immigration affinity, soft bonuses/penalties, diplomacy flavor, and law options over time.
- **Religion** — faiths with temples/shrines, rites, morale/cohesion effects, possible law hooks, and conflict potential between differing faiths or secular charters.
- Both can create **cooperation stories** (shared festivals, alliances) and **conflict stories** (schism, persecution laws, casus belli) without forcing every town into holy war.
- Buildings, civ meters (morale/population), and charter laws are valid integration points.

### Sequencing (large buildout)

| Phase | Scope |
|-------|--------|
| First Playable | Diegetic markers / shrine already in catalog OK as flavor; **no** full culture/religion sim |
| Next | Settlement culture tag + light cohesion; simple shrine/temple as morale building |
| Mid | Multiple cultures/faiths on map; laws; festival/rite events |
| Later | Schism, missionary pressure, deep interfaith diplomacy |

### Constraints

- Must not fight **regional specialization** or **phoenix settler** fantasy — identity supports place, doesn’t replace economy.
- Server-authoritative affiliation and law effects.
- Controller-readable: avoid opaque theology spreadsheets; show cohesion/faith pressure on meters people already watch.

## Open questions

1. Are cultures/faiths **preset** on the world, **player-founded**, or both?
2. Can a town be multi-faith / multi-culture with tension, or one primary identity?
3. How hard is religious conflict vs economic conflict (soft vs war-flag worthy)?
