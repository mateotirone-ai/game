# Progression

## Player (phoenix settler)

- Skills / familiarity from practice (soft caps; avoid hard class locks early).
- Reputation facets: merchant, builder, statesperson, warrior, pioneer.
- Property & capital: land claims, businesses, titles (persist across death per rules).
- Knowledge: recipes and tech contributions you personally unlocked or were taught.
- Death: gravestones / setbacks — not a full identity wipe.

## Settlement

- Tier thresholds (population, housing, food security, institutions).
- District unlocks and zoning.
- Shared tech and civic projects.

## Technology

Tech tree (data-driven) gates buildings, logistics, government forms, and military options. Research can be personal contribution into a settlement pool or era-wide discovery—decision recorded in `Design/Decisions/` when chosen.

## Pacing

Early game: survival clarity in minutes. Mid: economic identity in hours. Late: institutions and geopolitics over persistent eras.

## Anti-patterns

- Gear treadmills that obsolete phoenix continuity or settlement identity.
- Pay walls for civic power.
- Single optimal build order with no regional variance.
